TO KNOWLEDGE.

More than a building full of books - a home for
curious minds.

Greenwood public library has been at the heart of this
community since 1983. We believe everyone deserves free
access to knowledge, regardless of age, income, or
background. Our six sections cover everything from fiction
to digital media.

Beyond books, we host weekly events - reading
challenges for childrenm book clubs, digital literacy
workshops, and author meets. Our study rooms, free Wi-Fi,
and welcoming staff make Greenwood a place people
come back to, year after year.
